Which option should I choose?

Choose Brand X-Ray for a broad look at your early-stage brand. Choose a Private Working Session to work through one specific decision with your team. Choose Studio Hours when you need strategic or creative work produced.

What is the X-Ray sharing option?

Both X-Ray prices include a private recording and transcript. The $250 option means you're open to discussing excerpts for publication. Any public use requires your separate written approval.

How are Studio Hours scoped?

Prepaid hours define the scope. Send your brief below and I'll reply within two business days to confirm fit and priorities before work begins. Larger 20- and 40-hour sprints are available on request.

What comes after a live session?

You'll receive a recording and transcript. The conversation and next moves we discuss are the deliverable, rather than a custom deck or ongoing support. You're welcome to book again when another question comes up.

Can Studio Hours become a retainer?

Each block is a finite piece of work, and you can purchase another when there's another piece. These offers are project-based rather than embedded or fractional roles.
