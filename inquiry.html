That range means I can see upstream and downstream at the same time.

When someone presents a content problem, I can often tell that it is actually a positioning problem. When someone requests a rebrand, I can see when the business or product architecture underneath it is still unresolved. When someone asks for a campaign, I can identify whether the offer deserves one—or whether there are five unmade decisions preventing the work from succeeding in the first place.

You bring me the assignment.

I see the assignment underneath it.

Historically, when I saw those problems, I did not know how to leave them untouched. I embedded myself until I had repaired the whole system around the original assignment. That made me unusually valuable to early-stage companies. It also made every engagement more sprawling, difficult to price and emotionally consuming—and made the quality of my work increasingly dependent on other people's leadership, decision-making and pace.

These two formats are the result of finally understanding where my contribution is most valuable, what conditions allow me to do my best work and where access to my thinking needs a boundary.

A few useful boundaries.

Can you send a custom proposal first?

No. These formats replaced speculative proposals. If the need fits Studio Hours, the prepaid hours define the engagement. If the need is not yet defined, a Private Working Session is where we define it together.

Can Studio Hours become an open-ended retainer?

No. You can purchase another block when there is another finite piece of work. I am not taking on embedded, always-on or fractional CMO engagements.

Will you join recurring calls or manage our team?

Not within Studio Hours. If you need live judgment, book a session. If you need an embedded leader to own meetings, contractors and internal alignment, I am likely not the right fit for this chapter.

Who should attend a Private Working Session?

The people whose input matters and, whenever possible, the person who can make the decision. I do not want to add another layer of thinking between a team and the authority required to act.

What happens before you begin?

You send the existing context, constraints and desired outcome. I confirm that the work fits one of the two formats. No chemistry maze, unpaid audit or strategic proposal is required.

The invitation

Bring me the work. Or bring me the decision.
