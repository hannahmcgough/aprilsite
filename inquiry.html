How it works.

How is work scoped?

Prepaid hours define the scope. Your brief gives me the context to confirm fit within two business days, without a custom proposal or unpaid audit. We agree on priorities before work begins.

Can this become a retainer?

Each block is a finite piece of work. You can purchase another when there's another piece. These offers are project-based rather than embedded or fractional roles.

Do you offer an intro call?

The brief takes its place. If the need isn't defined yet, a Private Working Session is where we define it.

Will you join recurring meetings or manage a team?

Studio Hours are asynchronous, keeping the time focused on the work. For live judgment and team discussion, choose a Private Working Session.

Who should attend a Working Session?

The people whose input matters, including the person who can make the decision. Send context in advance so we can use the session to move forward.
