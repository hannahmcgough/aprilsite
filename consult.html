Why this exists

I love a good conversation. I also know that a conversation with the right person, at the right moment, can save a brand months of expensive wandering.

At this point, I can usually hear the pattern pretty quickly. What looks like a content problem might really be positioning. What sounds like a rebrand might be a product problem. The big campaign everyone is excited to fund might not be the next smartest investment. That clarity has value—and I can’t thoughtfully offer it to everyone for free.

I’m Hannah. For more than ten years, I’ve worked across the full, occasionally chaotic reality of building consumer brands.
