What shows up.

1

Positioning.

Who it's for, and why you over everyone else.

What I look for

whether a stranger could repeat your pitch; whether you’re being compared to the right competitors or the wrong ones; where “for everyone” is costing you the someone who would actually pay

2

Offer.

What you sell, and whether it's the right thing to sell first.

What I look for

the product people want versus the one you’re proudest of; where a first-time buyer hesitates, and what would shorten that; the hero product hiding in your lineup, and the ones crowding it.

3

Story.

What you say, and whether anyone repeats it.

What I look for

where your language is borrowed from your category making you sound like everyone else; the line your best customer would say to a friend, and whether you’re saying it.

4

Channel.

Where the brand shows up, and where it should stop showing up.

What I look for

the channels doing real work and the ones that aren't; effort spent where your customer isn’t, and the place you’re ignoring.

5

Price.

What it costs, and what that tells people.

What I look for

whether your price tells the same story as your brand; margins that cap growth, and discounts that train customers to wait.

You're welcome to reschedule once with at least 48 hours' notice. With less notice, the appointment remains chargeable and can't be moved.

You'll get the recording and transcript within 24 hours. At this time, I don't offer follow-up Q&A. If you enjoyed the session and would like another reading, simply book another appointment and we'll dive back in!
