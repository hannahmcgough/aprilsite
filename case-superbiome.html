Where it began: a test kit, customized formulas and a green skincare world.

Milieu centered on a mail-order skin swab, an app experience and customized skincare. The product was tangible. The company structure, mission, product strategy, membership logic and larger reason to exist were still moving.

The founders asked for a GTM playbook. Treating that as a standalone marketing assignment would have produced a polished plan for a business they had not yet collectively chosen.

The interruption was the work.

“The company needs its model resolved before any brand or GTM can sit on top.”

My first week became detective mode. I surfaced the consequential forks hiding beneath the brief, built a Strategic Foundation for Founder Ratification and coached the founders through genuinely viable—but mutually exclusive—futures.

This required more than presenting options. It required mediation, pattern recognition and enough conviction to inspire a new shared vision, help two founders make the call and return a coherent company structure to them before my original scope could begin.
